Where kids' smiles light up the room


Sehyog Foundation is a known champion of supporting child protection in India. Over the years, Sehyog Foundation has implemented a number of projects tackling different forms of abuse of children: trafficking, child marriage, child labour, sexual abuse and exploitation . Our work in this area revolves around prevention and timely response, focusing on developing robust child protection systems, strengthening them at the community-, state- and national levels and monitoring them to understand their efficacy. Sehyog Foundation has a proven track record in India of both delivering and influencing key policy decisions, such as the legislation on Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act (2012), Juvenile Justice Act and amendment of Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, and the enhancement of child protection services to safeguard vulnerable children.

Initiatives for Child Protection in India by Sehyog Foundation

  • Our Vision : End all forms of Violence, Abuse and Exploitation of Children in India

Key Child Protection Services

Sehyog Foundation core areas of work under Child Protection in India include interventions around Children in Street Situations, Child Protection System Strengthening by workforce development through a competency-based training at the local and sub-national levels, ending abuse and exploitation of children, promoting psychosocial well-being through mental health and psychosocial support, online safety of children, and advocating for the child policies in India .
